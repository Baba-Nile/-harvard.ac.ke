About us

Harvard Extention Training College is located in Nakuru and offers 50 courses across seven departments.

Practical training for working careers

We train students in health, business, engineering and ICT, hospitality, foreign languages, cosmetology, and fashion and design. Our approach is practical and industry based, backed by career guidance and support.

Vision

To inspire a new generation of learners equipped to shape the future, break barriers, and lead with confidence in a rapidly changing world.

Mission

Our mission is to extend opportunities for personal and professional development, preparing students to meet global challenges with skill and integrity.
