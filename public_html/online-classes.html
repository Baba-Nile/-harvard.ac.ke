Online learning

Students can access the learning programmes that the college has made available online. Sign in with your registered details to reach your notes, lessons and announcements.

How online learning works

  1. Choose a programme

    Find a programme marked as available online and select it.

  2. Sign in

    Use your registered full name. Your admission number is your password the first time.

  3. Create a private password

    After your first sign in you set your own password, then open your materials.
